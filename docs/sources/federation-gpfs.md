NSW council GPFS adapter: one council only (Federation Council), FY2024-25 General Purpose Financial Statements (year ended 30 June 2025) from the official Annual Financial Statements 2024-2025 PDF published on the council's Annual Financial Statements page. FY2024-25 is the latest audited GPFS published as at 2026-11-20; no FY2025-26 annual financial statements were linked, so no older year is substituted and the latest-available year is explicit. The entire official site is behind Akamai bot protection (plain clients get HTTP 403 'Access Denied' for the home page, sitemaps and every /files path), so the exact official pin is fetched through headless Chromium and the connector archives and SHA-256 verifies those bytes. The PDF embeds the Auditor-General's unmodified opinions on the General Purpose and Special Purpose Financial Statements (PDF pages 76-80 and 101-102). Parsed only when the printed $'000 unit is explicit and the Income Statement, B1-1 Total functions and activities, Statement of Comprehensive Income, Statement of Financial Position, Statement of Changes in Equity and Statement of Cash Flows reconcile exactly. Federation prints a nil net gain from the disposal of assets as a revenue component and a net loss from the disposal of assets as an expense component; total expenses is the documented bridge of the printed expenses-excluding-depreciation line plus printed depreciation/amortisation/impairment, equal to the printed B1-1 expense total; other comprehensive income is the single IPPE revaluation gain. B1-1 income includes grants and contributions, so B1-1 income equals the face total income, B1-1 operating result equals the net result, and B1-1 grants equal operating plus capital grants. Every row is a non-additive agency_detail line (provisional/local_government, unverified) with a distinct source id; no region/LGA allocation and no other council is ingested.
