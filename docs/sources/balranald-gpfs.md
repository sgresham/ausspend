NSW council GPFS adapter: one council only (Balranald Shire Council), FY2025-26 General Purpose Financial Statements (year ended 30 June 2026) from the official standalone Annual Financial Statements PDF published on the council's annual-reports page. The entire official site is behind Cloudflare bot management (plain clients get HTTP 403 for the home page, sitemaps and every upload path; only robots.txt is served), so the exact official pin is fetched through headless Chromium and the connector archives and SHA-256 verifies those bytes. The PDF embeds the signed Auditor-General section 417 opinions (PDF pages 77-78). The newest combined annual report on the same page is still FY2024-25, so no narrative annual report is substituted. Parsed only when the printed $'000 unit is explicit and the Income Statement, B1-1 Total functions and activities, Statement of Comprehensive Income, Statement of Financial Position, Statement of Changes in Equity and Statement of Cash Flows reconcile exactly. Balranald prints the net loss from the disposal of assets and the net share of interests in joint ventures and associates as expense components; total expenses is the documented bridge of the printed expenses-excluding-depreciation line plus printed depreciation/amortisation/impairment, equal to the printed B1-1 expense total; other comprehensive income is the revaluation gain plus a printed nil impairment/revaluation decrement. B1-1 income includes grants and contributions, so B1-1 income equals the face total income, B1-1 operating result equals the net result, and B1-1 grants equal operating plus capital grants. Every row is a non-additive agency_detail line (provisional/local_government, unverified) with a distinct source id; no region/LGA allocation and no other council is ingested.
