NSW council GPFS adapter: one council only (Greater Hume Shire Council), FY2025-26 General Purpose Financial Statements (year ended 30 June 2026) from the official audited Annual Financial Statements 2025-2026 PDF published on the council's Financial Statements page. This is the current audited FS presented to Council on 18 November 2026 and on exhibition until 25 November 2026; the older FY2024-25 financial statements linked on Documents and Policies are not substituted. The entire official site is behind Akamai bot protection (plain clients get HTTP 403 'Access Denied' for the home page and every /files path), so the exact official pin is fetched through headless Chromium and the connector archives and SHA-256 verifies those bytes. The PDF embeds the Auditor-General's unmodified opinions on the General Purpose and Special Purpose Financial Statements (PDF pages 88-89 and 108). Parsed only when the printed $'000 unit is explicit and the Income Statement, B1-1 Total functions and activities, Statement of Comprehensive Income, Statement of Financial Position, Statement of Changes in Equity and Statement of Cash Flows reconcile exactly. Greater Hume prints a net gain from the disposal of assets as a revenue component and a nil net loss from the disposal of assets as an expense component; total expenses is the documented bridge of the printed expenses-excluding-depreciation line plus printed depreciation/amortisation/impairment, equal to the printed B1-1 expense total; other comprehensive income is the single IPPE revaluation gain. B1-1 income includes grants and contributions, so B1-1 income equals the face total income, B1-1 operating result equals the net result, and B1-1 grants equal operating plus capital grants. Every row is a non-additive agency_detail line (provisional/local_government, unverified) with a distinct source id; no region/LGA allocation and no other council is ingested.
