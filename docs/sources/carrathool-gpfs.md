NSW council GPFS adapter: one council only (Carrathool Shire Council), FY2025-26 General Purpose Financial Statements (year ended 30 June 2026) from the official Annual Financial Statements PDF published under the council's Public Exhibition folder. The council's annual-report page and page sitemap still stop at the FY2023-24 narrative report, so the current audited GPFS is discovered from the council's own WordPress media index with a documented official fallback pin. The PDF embeds the signed, unmodified Auditor-General opinions for the GPFS and special purpose statements (dated 30 September 2026). The exact bytes are SHA-256 archived outside the repo, and parsed only when the printed $'000 unit is explicit and the Income Statement, B1-1 Total functions and activities, Statement of Comprehensive Income, Statement of Financial Position, Statement of Changes in Equity and Statement of Cash Flows reconcile exactly. Carrathool prints the net loss from the disposal of assets as an expense component; total expenses is the documented bridge of the printed expenses-excluding-depreciation line plus printed depreciation/amortisation/impairment, equal to the printed B1-1 expense total; other comprehensive income is the revaluation gain plus other movements. B1-1 income includes grants and contributions, so B1-1 income equals the face total income, B1-1 operating result equals the net result, and B1-1 grants equal operating plus capital grants. Every row is a non-additive agency_detail line (provisional/local_government, unverified) with a distinct source id; no region/LGA allocation and no other council is ingested.
